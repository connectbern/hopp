Hopp remote control

Control a teammate's Hopp app from here, if they allowed you to.

Sign in

Use your normal Hopp account on this server.

Nobody has allowed you yet

A teammate has to switch this on in their own Hopp app first:

Settings → Remote access → "Allow trusted people to control my Hopp remotely", then tick your name. Their app has to be a build that has this setting. Yours can be any version.

Call

"Join my call" brings them into the call you are in right now in your own Hopp app. They always join with microphone and camera off.

Their screen

They have to be in a call first. To see the screen, be in the same call with your own Hopp app.

App

Ends their call and reopens the app. It is back online after a few seconds.
